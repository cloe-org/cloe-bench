What explains it

  1. A tiny problem. 10 bins × 20 ℓ × 100 redshifts is microseconds of arithmetic; cloelib spends its time on about 1,600 JAX operations dispatched one at a time, each with a fixed cost.
  2. No perturbation theory. With linear bias, NLA, CLOE skips FAST-PT; switch on TATT + one-loop bias and CLOE takes —.
  3. An older cloelike. The June 2026 version rebuilt CAMB and the tracers for each probe, three times per call (fixed in September; today —).
  4. "No JAX" was JAX_DISABLE_JIT=1, a debugging mode that runs compiled code one primitive at a time, not cloelib without JAX.

Inside cloelib

cloelib main on its own, 8 CPUs unless stated. At small sizes its time is mostly JAX operations dispatched one at a time (about 2,000 per evaluation); with perturbation theory, the one-loop P(k, z) evaluated at every Limber point takes over as ℓ grows. CAMB, outside these times, costs about 5 s per cosmology.
